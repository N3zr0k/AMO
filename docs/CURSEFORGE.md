```markdown
# AMO - Ammunition Management Organizer

Ammo helper for hunters in WoW Forever. Works on its own.

* Warns when the quiver is about to run empty, when only a few rounds are left, and before a dungeon.
* Tooltip on ammo, quiver and ammo pouch: supply, rounds per minute, time until empty.
* Optionally equips the best usable ammo automatically.
* At a vendor, offers to refill the quiver with the strongest usable ammo.
* Can be switched off completely; does nothing for other classes.

Options: game options, category AMO. Commands: /amo
```
